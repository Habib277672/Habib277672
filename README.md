## 👋 About

I'm a full stack developer who cares about the details: interfaces that feel sharp and responsive, APIs that are clean and secure, and databases that are structured to grow.

Most of my work is for education businesses. I turn "we need a website" into a platform that **looks professional, loads fast, and is easy for your team to manage**.
